Highland Seed BankSeason notes · No. 09

Keeping the old barley alive.

Eleven growers across Orkney and the Western Isles save, sow and share seed of bere barley, a landrace grown here for over a thousand years.

Bere barley, Birsay
Oat sheaf, Uist
Rye seed tray, Lewis

Seed is kept by being planted.

Frozen vaults hold samples, but a variety adapts only in a living field. Each member plants a quarter hectare and returns a tenth of the harvest to the shared store.

"The Bere ripens in ninety days. Nothing else will, this far north."Mairi Gunn · grower, Westray
