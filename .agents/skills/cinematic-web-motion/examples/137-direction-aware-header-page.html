Est. 2008 · Bergen and Leeds

Houses that are grown before they are built.

Halden & Rowe design small public buildings in cross-laminated timber: libraries, village halls, school extensions. Every beam is drawn knowing which tree it comes from.

Practice

Nine architects, two sawmills.

We work directly with two Norwegian sawmills so that cutting lists are agreed before drawings are final. Offcuts return as cladding, shelving and benches.

Method

We weigh every wall.

Each project is carbon-counted at three stages and published. Askrigg stores 41 tonnes of CO2 in its frame; the figure is painted inside the entrance.
