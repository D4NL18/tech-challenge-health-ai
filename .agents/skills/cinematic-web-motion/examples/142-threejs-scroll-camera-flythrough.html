Vale Alto TrailKm 0

Forty kilometres, one valley.

A guided traverse of the Vale Alto, from the grazing meadows to the old market town at the end of the line.

Km 8 · The meadows

Start where the grass is still wet.

Huts open at six. The first stretch is flat and soft, with two stream crossings and a dairy that sells warm cheese from a bench.

Km 17 · The pine ridge

The path climbs, then lets you breathe.

Four hundred metres of gain through spruce planted by the cooperative in 1962. Wind drops here; most walkers stop for lunch at the stone table.

Km 29 · The pass

A saddle at 1,980 metres.

Open ground, long views, and a weather station you may use to dry your socks. Descent follows the old mule route into the town.

Beds for 14 each night. Book before April.
