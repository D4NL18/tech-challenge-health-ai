Cost

How much will it actually cost me?

A typical three-bed semi comes to £11,200 before the government grant and £4,200 after it. That includes the heat pump, a new 200 litre cylinder, any radiators we need to swap, and commissioning.

You get a fixed quote after a one-hour survey. It does not change on the day.

Fit

Will it work in a house built in 1930?

Usually yes. Insulation helps but is rarely a precondition. We measure heat loss room by room and size the unit from that, not from the age of the house.

About one in ten surveys ends with "loft insulation first", and we say so even though it means no sale.

Comfort

Will my radiators still get hot?

They will get warm, around 45 °C instead of 70 °C, and stay that way for longer. Most people notice steadier rooms rather than a hot burst followed by cold.

Running costs

Is it cheaper than gas to run?

On our standard heat pump tariff, customers in the Lakes average £1,050 a year for heating and hot water, against £1,380 on gas for the same houses in 2024.

The saving shrinks if you stay on the default electricity tariff. We help you switch.

Install

How long are we without heating?

Two to three days for most homes. We fit the outdoor unit first, then swap the boiler on day two. We leave you a fan heater and a flask if it is January.

Noise

Is the outdoor unit noisy?

About 42 decibels at one metre, roughly a quiet fridge. We position it at least a metre from neighbours' windows and show you the planned spot with chalk before drilling.

Grants

Who fills in the grant forms?

We do. You sign once. The grant is taken off your invoice, so you never front the money and wait for a refund.

Aftercare

What if something stops working?

Every system has a seven-year warranty and a named engineer. Call before 3 pm and someone is with you the next working day, usually the same one who installed it.
