Fieldfare Pottery · Kiln No. 4

    Pots fired once, the slow way.

    A four-person workshop in the Calder valley making tableware from local stoneware clay, wood-fired for 38 hours at a time.

    Next firing: 14 June
    Open yard Saturdays, 10 to 4
    1

    02 · Throwing

    Every bowl starts at 900 grams.

    Clay is weighed, wedged for four minutes and thrown without a template. Rims are checked with a thumb, not a gauge.

    62pieces per wheel day
    7%lost in drying
    2

    03 · Glazing

    Ash from our own fires.

    Beech and ash residue is sieved, washed and mixed into the glaze. Each batch runs slightly greener or browner than the last, and we keep the test tiles.

    Cone 10 reduction
    Fourteen glaze recipes in rotation
    3

    04 · Firing

    Thirty-eight hours, no sleep for the stokers.

    Shifts of two feed the firebox every eight minutes. The kiln reaches 1,290 degrees before the door is bricked shut to cool for three days.

    Wood used per firing: 1.8 tonnes
    4

    05 · Visit

    Unload day is open to everyone.

    Come when the kiln is opened. Pieces are priced on the spot and the seconds shelf is half price.
