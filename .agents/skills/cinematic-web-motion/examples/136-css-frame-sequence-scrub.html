The keeper's lamp is the only light on the water.

Boats from Port Selene leave on the first usable tide, long before there is a horizon to steer by.

The headland turns from black to rust.

The beam is switched off by hand, never by timer, once the keeper can read the harbour chart without it.

06:30 · Sun clears the ridge

The trawler Annick is already past the breakwater.

Four crew, nine hours, a quota of 1.2 tonnes of whiting. The wake is what you see first.

07:15 · Day shift

Quay opens for the market at eight.

Stalls are set up in the cold and ice is delivered by the pallet. The ferry leaves for the island at nine.

Timetable for the island crossing.
