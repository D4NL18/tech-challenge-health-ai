Ashby & Lowe, furniture makers, Todmorden

From sketch to dining table in nine weeks.

Every piece is built by one maker whose name goes on the underside. This is what the nine weeks look like.

  1. 01
    Week 1

    We visit and measure the room.

    Light, doorways, the dog, the four chairs you already own. We leave with a tape-measured sketch and a budget range, not a quote yet.

  2. 02
    Weeks 2 to 3

    You choose from the boards.

    Six offcuts of ash, oak, walnut and cherry arrive by post, each oiled on one half. Live with them on your windowsill for a fortnight before deciding.

  3. 03
    Week 4

    A full-size mock-up in cardboard.

    Most mistakes cost nothing at this stage. We stand a cardboard table in your room, you sit at it for a meal, and we adjust the height by a centimetre or two.

  4. 04
    Weeks 5 to 8

    Four weeks at the bench.

    Boards are joined by hand, left to rest for a day between glue-ups, and planed until the grain lines run unbroken across the top. You get a photo every Friday.

  5. 05
    Week 9

    Delivered, levelled, signed.

    Two of us carry it in, shim the feet, and sign the underside in pencil. Your oil-finish care sheet is in the drawer. We call after one month to ask how it is settling.
