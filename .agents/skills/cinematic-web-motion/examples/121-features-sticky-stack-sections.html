Tarn & EmberRoasters, Bristol

Four hands between the farm and your cup.

We roast in 12 kg batches above a former tannery on Welsh Back. Here is what happens to a bag of coffee before it reaches you, in the order it happens.

01
Sourcing

We pay the farm before the harvest.

Each spring we fix a price per kilo with six families around Huila, Colombia, and prepay 40% of it. The money arrives when the cherries are still green, which is when it is actually needed.

  • Farm-gate price, 2025$4.10 / kg green
  • Commodity benchmark$2.65 / kg
  • Producers6 families
02
Roasting

Eleven minutes, watched by a person.

Every batch is profiled by ear and by eye. First crack tells us when to drop the heat; the cooling tray tells us when we got it right. We cup the first and last bag of each day, and we bin the ones that miss.

  • Batch size12 kg
  • Drop temperature205 °C
  • Rest before bagging5 days
03
Brew lab

Recipes you can repeat on a Tuesday.

Each coffee ships with a card: dose, water weight, grind setting for the five grinders we see most often, and a time. If your cup tastes thin, the card tells you which number to change first.

  • Pour-over ratio1 : 16.5
  • Water93 °C
  • Total time2 min 50
