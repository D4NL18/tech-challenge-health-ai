Houses that listen to the street.

Studio · Porto, since 2009Nine architects who build small: schools, courtyard houses and the occasional bakery. Most of our work sits within 30 kilometres of the studio so we can visit it in the rain.

We draw the section before the plan.

MethodA building is a series of rooms with light entering at a certain angle. We start with how a morning moves across a wall, then decide where the walls go.

Every project starts with a week of observation on site and a model in cardboard that clients are encouraged to cut apart.
